CB Insights

The five things that matter

  1. Beachhead = med spas, proof-case = dental. Med spas are the fastest-growing segment (+17.9% locations 2022→2023, AmSpa), carry premium pricing ($4–5k/mo vs dental $1.5–3k), are cosmetic-first (lighter PHI/compliance load), and are the least saturated at the managed-service tier. Dental is the largest raw base (~135k) and our existing outbound is already there, but it is the most incumbent-crowded (Weave, Arini, RevenueWell) — use it for the first before/after case study, scale on med spas.
  2. The money hook is protecting demand they already bought. Practices spend $100–$500 in ad spend to make the phone ring, then miss ~1 in 3 of those calls; ~78% of callers who hit voicemail hang up and ~85% never call back vendor-est. The AI front desk recovers the highest-ROI dollar in the clinic — the patient whose call was already paid for.
  3. Compliance is a moat, not a blocker. HIPAA/BAA, TCPA/10DLC for outbound, two-party recording consent, and AI-disclosure law (CA AB 2905, $500/call) are all configurational. Our Retell stack already signs BAAs. This setup work is exactly what a small clinic will not do itself — it is the reason to hire a managed operator.
  4. The market's objection is "flaky AI grift," and it is real. Owners publicly report AI bots that made patients repeat their name and DOB and degraded over months. Our entire wedge is human-tuned, monitored, escalates-to-staff — sold as a service, not a tool you babysit.
  5. TAM is not the constraint; message leverage is. With ~164k reachable practices, volume outreach is viable. The job is to niche down per campaign (one sub-segment, one state) so the report hook lands specific.

Offer — the value equation

Increase the top two, decrease the bottom two.

↑ Dream outcome

Sell the recovered revenue, not "AI answering." Name it with weight: the Clinic Front-Desk OS — every call answered, qualified, and booked, plus your dormant patients reactivated. The promise is "stop paying to make the phone ring and then not answering it."

↑ Perceived likelihood

Pay-on-result pilot: "we recover N booked new-patient calls in 30 days before you pay setup," plus an SLA credit-back. Framing: "we take the tech risk, you take the volume risk." Human-monitored and managed — the direct answer to the "flaky AI" objection.

↓ Time delay

Live in days, first recovered booking in week one. State both numbers. Speed to first result is the whole sell in a speed-to-lead market.

↓ Effort & sacrifice

Pure done-for-you: they hand over booking criteria + PMS access; we build, host, monitor, and carry the compliance (BAA, 10DLC, disclosure). Every step pushed back on the clinic is a discount we take without getting paid.

The guarantee (with teeth)

"We recover a booked new patient in your first 30 days or you don't pay setup." Define "booked new patient" tightly, in writing (a first-time caller who scheduled and is confirmed in your PMS). A guarantee pegged to their conversion inherits a variable we don't control — so guarantee the recovered booking (which our answering controls), not their downstream close.

Copy — lead magnet + the report-interview outreach

Lead-magnet mechanic — give away the diagnosis

The Missed-Call Revenue Leak report (our lead-leak-finder). Show the prospect their own leak with their own data: call their line after hours, log what happened, and put a dollar figure on it for a practice their size in their state. The pain is the pitch. CTA = "want the findings?" — never "book a call." Do not covertly record their live system and send it back — it reads as a gotcha and has recording-consent exposure. Build the magnet from public data + what they hand you.

The outreach — Heidar's report-interview method (person-led, no pitch)

Reach out as Mikail / Ahmad doing research, not as an agency. Outcome language, never lead with "AI". Personalize by state + clinic. All copy through /humanizer, no dashes/semicolons.

Subject: featuring {{ClinicName}} in the report? Hi {{FirstName}}, I'm putting together a short report on how {{State}} {{med spas / dental practices}} handle after-hours and missed calls, since it's where most clinics quietly lose new patients. Yours came up when I was looking around {{City}}. Would you be open to a 30 to 40 minute interview for it? No pitch, nothing to buy. I'll send you the finished report on your clinic and the clip is yours to keep. If you're up for it, just reply and I'll get you scheduled. {{Mikail / Ahmad}}

Honest risks

Regulatory — must-do, not optional. A call-handling agent touches PHI on nearly every call → a signed BAA is mandatory with each clinic and with every subprocessor (Retell signs BAAs — the enabler). Outbound reactivation needs TCPA consent + 10DLC A2P registration (healthcare = high-scrutiny vetting) → scope outbound to the clinic's own consented patients, not cold. Two-party recording consent in ~15 states + AI-disclosure (CA AB 2905, $500/undisclosed call) → build "you're speaking with an AI assistant" into the opener by default. sources: Retell, Infobip, Dialzara, Thoughtly
Delivery — the "grift" risk is real. Owners publicly report AI bots that made patients repeat name/DOB and degraded over months ("this isn't working… the longer conversations go, the worse it performs"). If our AI plateaus, we become the objection. Mitigation = the entire model: human-tuned, monitored, escalates clinical/edge cases to staff, and we own the SLA.
Saturation (opinion). Dental is crowded (Weave, Arini, RevenueWell). The bigger structural threat is the CRM platforms (Boulevard $80M/$800M, Zenoti ~$1.5B) bundling AI reception into a seat clinics already pay for. Counter by leading med spas (less crowded at the managed tier) and winning on service depth + compliance, which a platform bolt-on can't match.
Data quality (opinion). Nearly every missed-call / loss-rate / CPA stat in this brief is vendor/agency-sourced — there is no neutral academic benchmark. Present them as ranges, and in a live pitch lead with the prospect's own numbers (from the leak-finder), not ours.
Highest-leverage move. Nail the med-spa managed + compliance + local-proof wedge and get one lighthouse before/after case study. Everything downstream (the report hook, the guarantee, the content) gets sharper the moment there is one named clinic with a recovered-revenue number.

Glossary — talk to the market without sounding like a tourist

The call → book → reactivate flow

Inbound call ──▶ AI answers (with AI disclosure) ──▶ qualifies (new vs existing, reason, insurance) ──▶ books into the PMS / calendar ──▶ clinical or urgent question? ──▶ escalate to staff Dormant patient ──▶ consented reactivation text/call ──▶ re-book ──▶ CRM/calendar/payments stay in sync
PMS
Practice Management System — the clinic's core software (Dentrix, Open Dental, Eaglesoft in dental; Boulevard, Zenoti, Mangomint in med spas). The AI must book into it.
DSO
Dental Support Organization — a group that owns/operates multiple practices. Buys centrally; found on LinkedIn (unlike solo owners).
Med spa
Medical spa — aesthetic clinic offering injectables, lasers, skin treatments under medical supervision. Cosmetic-first, cash-pay, premium.
Injectables
Botox/neuromodulators + dermal fillers — the volume driver of med-spa revenue (9.48M + 5.29M procedures in 2023, ASPS).
Recall / reactivation
Bringing lapsed or dormant patients back in. "Haven't seen you in X months" — the second half of our product.
No-show
A booked patient who doesn't arrive. Reducing it is a headline metric for the CRM platforms.
Speed-to-lead
Time from inquiry to first response. The 5-minute rule (MIT/InsideSales, ~2007) and HBR 2011 — foundational but dated; the vertical-specific missed-call data is the current evidence.
LTV
Lifetime value of a patient — dental ~$6,700, med spa ~$7,800 blended. The number behind reactivation ROI.
PHI · BAA
Protected Health Information · Business Associate Agreement — the HIPAA contract required because the AI handles patient data. We sign one per clinic and per subprocessor.
TCPA · 10DLC
Telephone Consumer Protection Act · the A2P texting registration. Governs outbound reactivation — requires prior consent + registered messaging.
CPA
Cost per acquired patient — $80–$500 dental, $200–$300 med spa. The number our "recover demand you already bought" pitch undercuts.
